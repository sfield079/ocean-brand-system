# Ocean RCS brand system

For every Ocean deliverable (deck, report, proposal, letter, contract, web page,
micro-app screen, card, email signature, signage, chart or graphic), use
https://github.com/sfield079/ocean-brand-system. Read AGENTS.md, then
brand/BRAND-SYSTEM.md, brand/decisions.md and brand/typography.md. If the
repository is unavailable, use the uploaded copies and the Ocean Style Guide
2026; never claim to read inaccessible sources.

The Ocean Style Guide 2026 is the Brand Bible and is binding. Use the full
nine-color palette through the approved two-color pairings only, with one pairing
per page or section. Honeydew is the off-white canvas, and White is for print
and formal documents. Use Stack Sans Headline in four weights (300/400/500/700),
with all caps Bold or Light at +0.25 em. Legal documents use Times New Roman.
Email signatures fall back to Arial.

Presentations and reports are different systems; follow the deck recipes for
any length. Formal documents are plain and institutional: logo on page 1,
graphic mark on continuation pages, no decoration. Logo minimum sizes are in
BRAND-SYSTEM.md §16. In charts, Sage is context and Cedar is the focus, and
every number on a data shape is Honeydew Bold.

Images may come from Ocean photos, licensed stock or top-rated generators, and
unlicensed photos are excluded. Label AI images "Representative". Never invent
facts, metrics or promises. Inspect every rendered page against
output/reference/ before delivery. Automated PASS is not visual QA.
