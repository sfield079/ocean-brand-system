# Ocean editorial design system

White, spacious and precise. Adopt OceanAshers' type scale and open composition,
OceanOP081926's compact editorial hierarchy, and oceanrcs.com's white / Blackmoss
/ Sage balance. Remove patterns, watermarks, synthetic imagery and decoration.

Hierarchy: original logo, title, evidence, explanation, source. One dominant
composition per page. Use whitespace to focus content, not oversized empty boxes.

## Color roles

`color-system.json` is canonical. Keep approved hex values; improve their usage.

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | White #FFFFFF | Covers and content pages |
| Primary ink | Blackmoss #0B1617 | Titles, body and numbers |
| Secondary ink | Cedar #1B4039 | Small labels and supporting copy |
| Accent | Sage #618C7C | Large emphasis, rules, chart series, optional cover |
| Dark structure | Peacock #102426 | Small table headers and chart series |
| Rules | Fog #D5DED9 | Thin separators |
| Tint | Mist #EEF2F0 | Occasional table stripes only |

Sage on white is too light for small body copy. Use Cedar or Blackmoss for small
labels, captions and footers. Natural photographs and original black logo art
retain their colors. Honeydew/Sprig/Olive/Citron/Crimson are not the default
publication palette, following the reference chat. Replace OceanAshers' older
teal with current Sage. Do not apply a green wash to images.

## Typography and spacing

Stack Sans Headline is mandatory. Bold for major titles and numbers, Regular
for prose, Medium/SemiBold for labels. Sentence-case titles. Uppercase only for
short labels. No stretching, condensation or artificial letter spacing in prose.

Slides: 13.333 × 7.5 inches, 0.6-inch margins, 0.3–0.5-inch column gaps,
48 pt cover, 32 pt titles, 17 pt body, 13–16 pt tables, 10 pt footers.
Documents: Letter by default, 0.55-inch margins, 30 pt title, 18 pt sections,
11 pt body, 10 pt tables, 9 pt footers. Repeat table headers across pages and
keep headings with their first paragraph. A4 is available when requested.

## Layout vocabulary

- Cover: horizontal logo, one large title, short subtitle and date. White default.
- Divider: section number and title. Optional real photograph, no fabricated art.
- Statement: dominant type and short support, optionally one source photograph.
- Comparison: open columns with thin top rules, no heavy filled panels.
- Metrics: aligned numbers, labels and sources, no decorative card frames.
- Evidence: native editable tables/charts, visible units and qualifications.
- Case study: factual scope/status and supplied photograph when available.
- Team: concise names, roles, verified experience; no fake avatars.
- Proposal: decision summary, scope, assumptions/exclusions, schedule, commercial
  terms and next steps. Never fabricate prices or promises to fill the design.
- Report: purpose, method, findings, evidence, limitations and next actions.

## Acceptance

PDFs use selectable text and embedded Stack Sans Headline fonts. PPTX evidence
remains editable. Install the fonts on any machine editing it. Inspect all pages
for clipping, overlap, low contrast, unnatural breaks and inconsistent rhythm.
Do not confuse a successful export or automated PASS with visual approval.

No AI elements by default. A later explicit request may authorize a labeled
concept illustration; it does not authorize fake evidence or text backgrounds.
