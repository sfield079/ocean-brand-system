# Ocean design system (index)

The earlier "white, spacious editorial" system that lived here is **superseded**
by the approved Ocean Brand System (26 September 2026). Use:

| Need | File |
|---|---|
| Binding visual authority | `brand/Ocean_StyleGuide_2026.pdf` |
| Approved decisions and document profiles | `brand/decisions.md` |
| Typography rules | `brand/typography.md` |
| Every surface: web, apps, decks, reports, formal and legal, cards, email, signage, charts, icons, graphics, logo sizing | `brand/BRAND-SYSTEM.md` |
| Tokens | `tokens/ocean.tokens.json`, `tokens/build/` |
| What approved output looks like | `output/reference/`, `surfaces/*/` |

Summary of what changed from the old file:
- The full nine-color palette and the approved two-color pairings replace
  "white pages, Blackmoss text and sparse Sage accents". The default deck cover
  is Sage with Honeydew and code-generated tonal texture. Honeydew is the
  off-white canvas, and White is kept for print and formal documents.
- Subtle procedural wave textures, chamfer frames and diamond counters from
  the guide are part of the brand, used with restraint.
- Images may come from Ocean photography, licensed stock or top-rated
  generators. They must be recorded in the image manifest, and unlicensed photos
  are excluded.
- Legal documents use Times New Roman. Everything else uses Stack Sans Headline.
