# Ocean Pro Forma Kit

Status: controlling financial-model presentation guidance, added 4 Oct 2026. Applies to detailed pro formas in decks, proposals, reports, Vault, Wave and investor materials.

## 1. Purpose

The pro-forma kit makes detailed economics readable without inventing figures or hiding assumptions. It is for analysis, diligence and decision support. It is not a guarantee of returns.

## 2. Required structure

Use these modules as needed:

- Executive economics summary.
- Verified inputs and open assumptions.
- Revenue bridge.
- Cost stack.
- Incentive and credit logic.
- Capital stack.
- Payback, IRR, DSCR or other returns only when verified by the user or source evidence.
- Sensitivity table.
- Risk and dependency register.
- Sources, assumptions and limitations.
- Appendix with calculation detail.

## 3. Visual mode

Use the financial/pro-forma product mode: Honeydew or White field, Blackmoss text, Peacock headers, Sage rules and Crimson semantic accents.

Crimson may mark total revenue, critical impact, missing required assumptions, risk flags, stale inputs, pricing deadlines and investor-decision figures. Use at most one Crimson emphasis per slide, page, card, row, chart or screen state.

## 4. Tables

- Keep row labels specific and plain.
- Show source, date and assumption basis near the table.
- Use tabular numbers and align decimal places.
- Mark forecast, target, illustrative and placeholder values visibly.
- Never hide unresolved inputs. Use `[TBD]` and list the missing item.
- Do not include internal margin, vendor sourcing costs or private cost-plus logic in external materials.

## 5. Charts

- Use one message per chart.
- Use Sage for context and Cedar for the focus.
- Use Crimson only for the semantic flagged value.
- Include source and assumption notes.
- Put detailed data in an appendix table when a chart simplifies it.

## 6. Deliverable formats

- Deck: one decision slide plus appendix modules.
- Proposal PDF: summary, assumptions, pricing/economics table, risks and appendix.
- Report PDF: full method, assumptions, scenario analysis and source register.
- Product screen: mobile summary first, then expandable assumptions, sensitivity and export.

## 7. QA

Before delivery, check:

- No invented projects, customers, contracts, incentives, pricing, savings, revenue, returns, licenses or credentials.
- Every key number has source, date, basis and status.
- Placeholder values remain `[TBD]` in draft and are blocked in release.
- Forecasts and targets are labeled.
- Internal margin and vendor cost detail are removed from external materials.
- PDF fonts are embedded and every page is readable on phone-size preview and full-size desktop.
