# Investor deck rules (in addition to root AGENTS.md)

- 10–13 slides for a teaser; 12–16 plus appendix for a full deck.
- Every slide footer shows CONFIDENTIAL (OceanDeck default `confidential: true`).
- Forward-looking numbers must be labeled Projected, Targeted, or Illustrative on the slide.
- Add this footnote to any financial slide: "Forward-looking and subject to diligence; not a guarantee of returns."
- Use cautious language: "targeted", "potential", "subject to diligence". Never "guaranteed".
- Use a decision-oriented order suited to the brief: cover, thesis/problem,
  platform/model, evidence, deployment, economics, team, ask and appendix.
  Do not force unsupported proof or unnecessary pages into every deck.
- No internal margins, cost-plus detail, or vendor pricing on investor slides.
