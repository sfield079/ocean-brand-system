# Investor deck rules (in addition to root AGENTS.md)

- Length follows the recipes (`surfaces/decks/recipes.json`, BRAND-SYSTEM.md §8): the 10-slide
  client presentation for a teaser, the 15-slide recipe plus appendix for a full deck.
- Set `classification: 'investor'` and a status (`draft` until approved). Every interior slide footer
  then reads, for example, "Ocean RCS · Confidential · For discussion only · Draft · Do not use", and an
  "Important notice" slide is added before the close automatically (decisions.md Part 10).
- Forward-looking numbers must be labeled Projected, Targeted or Illustrative on the slide.
- Add this footnote to any financial slide: "Forward-looking and subject to diligence; not a guarantee of returns."
- Use cautious language: "targeted", "potential", "subject to diligence". Never "guaranteed".
- Use a decision-oriented order suited to the brief: cover, thesis/problem, platform/model, evidence,
  deployment, economics, team, ask and appendix. Do not force unsupported proof or unnecessary pages.
- No internal margins, cost-plus detail or vendor pricing on investor slides.
